265.29 m

Ara hesap dökümü (Excel formülleriyle)
İletken: 3/0 PIGEON | Bölge: 2 | ar: 150 m | Liz=0.5 m
Max Sehim Hali: +45°C, çıplak (Buz=0 Rüz=0) (otomatik) | DfDs5 — Geniş travers (E)

Pn (%100 buz/rüz) = 0.3429 kg   p0 (çıplak) = 0.3429 kg   sinα=0.10886
Tn (max sehim) = 349.95 kg   Tn₊₅ = 540.94 kg

Df = 1.74 m    Ds = 3.60 m

— Yatay kol (adf) —
fmax = 4·(Df − U/150)² − Liz = 4·(1.74 − 0.230)² − 0.5 = 8.6204 m
adf = √(8·Tn·fmax / Pn) = √(8·349.95·8.6204 / 0.3429) = 265.29 m

— Düşey kol (ads) —
f₊₅ = ((Ds − U/150)/(2·sinα)) − Liz = ((3.60 − 0.230)/(2·0.10886)) − 0.5 = 14.9786 m
ads = √(8·Tn₊₅·f₊₅ / p0) = √(8·540.94·14.9786 / 0.3429) = 434.78 m

amax = min(265.29 , 434.78) = 265.29 m
